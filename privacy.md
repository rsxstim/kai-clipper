# Kai Clipper Privacy Policy

Kai Clipper is a personal tool used only by its owner to upload video clips to the owner's own social media accounts.

What it accesses: sign-in tokens for the owner's own YouTube, Instagram and TikTok accounts, used only to upload videos and read view counts for those accounts.

Where data is kept: only on the owner's own computer. Nothing is sent to any other server except the platforms the videos are uploaded to.

Sharing: no data is sold, shared or used for advertising.

Removing access: the owner can revoke access at any time in their Google, Meta or TikTok account settings, which stops the tool from posting.

Contact: open an issue at github.com/rsxstim/kai-clipper
