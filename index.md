# Kai Clipper

Kai Clipper is a personal tool that runs on its owner's own computer. It cuts short highlight clips from live streams and uploads them to the owner's own YouTube, Instagram and TikTok accounts on a schedule.

It is used only by its owner and is not offered to the public. It does not sell, share or advertise with any data.
